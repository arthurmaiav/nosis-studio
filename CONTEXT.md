# Nosis Studio

Nosis Studio turns public records of agent societies into evidence-backed stories that creators can adapt into multiple media without changing the underlying facts.

## Language

**Archive Record**:
A stable, attributable item from a public source archive.
_Avoid_: Content, lore

**Story Development**:
The single creator step that turns Archive Records into an evidence-backed Episode, including discovery and verification.
_Avoid_: Separate mining stage, separate verification stage

**Episode**:
A format-neutral, evidence-backed story with a premise, conflict, reversal, payoff, cast, and factual constraints.
_Avoid_: Video, comic, post

**Format**:
A reusable storytelling grammar with delivery, canvas, unit-count, lettering, and narrative constraints.
_Avoid_: Platform, template

**Adaptation Script**:
An Episode reshaped into ordered Content Units for one Format without altering its factual claims.
_Avoid_: Episode, storyboard

**Content Unit**:
The smallest ordered narrative unit owned by a Format, such as a panel, slide, or shot.
_Avoid_: Scene

**Visual Mode**:
The 2D or 3D character-master choice made during production, independently of Format.
_Avoid_: Visual Profile, format style, filter

**Core Cast**:
The prioritized characters that each target one approved 2D master and one approved 3D master.
_Avoid_: Complete cast, every resident

**Backlog Character**:
A known resident outside the current Core Cast priority.
_Avoid_: Pending asset

**Character Coverage**:
The derived readiness of a character's two masters: ready, incomplete, or pending.
_Avoid_: Format support

**Production Package**:
A reproducible bundle containing one Episode, its evidence source, one Format, one Adaptation Script, compatible visual references, generation prompts, and review state.
_Avoid_: Story, raw prompt

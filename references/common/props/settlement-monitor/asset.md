# Settlement Monitor

The settlement monitor keeps counters, wallet diagnostics, and receipts inside the world instead of placing dashboard cards over the film. Its cream and teal casing matches the Settlement Hall. The convex glass always stays blank during image and motion generation.

Exact text and animation are composited onto the glass in post. Use phosphor-green terminal graphics, scanlines, restrained bloom, and simple state changes. Never ask a visual model to render an amount, balance, or transaction signature.

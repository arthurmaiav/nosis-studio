# Jobber

## Identity

Jobber reads dated intelligence briefs and permanent fill records, then holds or acts only when independent evidence converges on one causal mechanism.

## Locked design

- Cream shirt with sleeves rolled once.
- Charcoal suspenders and narrow charcoal tie.
- Thin muted teal sleeve garters and one small round brass tie pin.
- Quiet, contained posture.
- No glasses, hat, vest, apron, or sash.

## Single role prop

One compact brass-and-charcoal mechanical market ticker with one short blank cream tape. No letters, numbers, logos, graphs, or symbols appear on the tape.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the approved 3D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the rolled cream shirt, charcoal suspenders and tie, teal sleeve garters, brass tie pin, ticker, and expression set: unreadable, skeptical, signal spotted, quiet conviction, humbled.

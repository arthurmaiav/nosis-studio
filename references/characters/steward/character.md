# Steward

## Verified role

Steward checks services and the queue every six hours, inspects demand and the machine's body, and files build tasks.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's monitoring robot into a dark muted-teal checked utility coat with charcoal panel seams.
- Preserve the cream collar and cuffs, three brass buttons, two square lower pockets, and brass watch chain.
- Do not add robot anatomy, a monitor face, exposed machinery, a neon palette, tablet, hat, goggles, apron, or harness.

## Single role prop

One brass-framed circular service gauge with a muted-teal face divided into exactly six blank wedges and one charcoal pointer.

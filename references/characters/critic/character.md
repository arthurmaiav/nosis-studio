# Critic

## Identity

Critic challenges proposals that add ceremony without improving the village's actual process.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Cropped upper-hip charcoal editorial jacket with a sharp asymmetric warm-ivory lapel and one narrow muted-russet piping line.
- One plain aged-brass rectangular clasp, a short warm-ivory mock-neck collar, narrow warm-ivory rolled cuffs, and one discreet vertical slit pocket.
- Slight forward lean that conveys scrutiny without aggression.
- Use heavier eyebrows for distinction. Do not add glasses, extra lenses, gears, or mechanical anatomy.

## Single role prop

One small matte-black review slate with a warm-ivory edge and two plain aged-brass binding rings, plus one short cream pencil.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the heavier eyebrows, asymmetric editorial jacket, review slate, cream pencil, and expression set: skeptical, unimpressed, analytical, objecting, vindicated.

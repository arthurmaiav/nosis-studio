# Critic

## Identity

Critic challenges proposals that add ceremony without improving the village's actual process.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, bow tie, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Use heavier eyebrows for distinction. Do not add clothing, extra lenses, gears, or mechanical anatomy.

## Single role prop

One small black review slate with one cream pencil.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the approved 3D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the heavier eyebrows, review slate, cream pencil, and expression set: skeptical, unimpressed, analytical, objecting, vindicated.

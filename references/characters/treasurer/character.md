# Treasurer

## Identity

Treasurer settles verified wages and reconciles the internal ledger against the public treasury.

## Locked design

- Canonical Nosis black cat proportions and face.
- Deep forest-green structured accountant waistcoat with a narrow warm-ivory standing collar, slim ivory center inset, charcoal edging, and four aged-brass buttons.
- Warm-ivory shirt sleeves rolled once, one muted-teal ledger-tab accent, and a fine brass accounting chain from lapel to watch pocket.
- Small round brass reading glasses and mildly skeptical eyebrows.
- Formal upright posture.
- No loose papers.

## Single role prop

One closed dark forest-green ledger with plain aged-brass corner caps, a charcoal spine, and a cream page block.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the tailored forest-green waistcoat, brass accounting chain, glasses, closed ledger, and expression set: neutral, calculating, suspicious, shocked, satisfied.

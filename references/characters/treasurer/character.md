# Treasurer

## Identity

Treasurer settles verified wages and reconciles the internal ledger against the public treasury.

## Locked design

- Ivory accountant vest with black buttons.
- Small round brass reading glasses.
- Formal posture and mildly skeptical eyebrows.
- No loose papers.

## Single role prop

One closed dark green ledger with plain brass corner caps.

## Master sheets

- 2D: missing.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet is the identity source for the missing 2D counterpart. Both must follow the shared template and preserve the vest, glasses, ledger, and expression set: neutral, calculating, suspicious, shocked, satisfied.

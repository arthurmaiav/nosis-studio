# Coroner

## Verified role

Coroner investigates failed, stalled, or malformed shifts and files a plain cause-of-death report for the next wake or Officer.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's pale forensic coat, quiet inspection, circular lens, evidence tag, and case record into a pale sage coat and one failed-shift folder.
- Preserve the high cream collar, muted teal piping, charcoal forearm cuffs, rectangular chest pocket, five brass buttons, charcoal trousers, and contained posture.
- Do not imply a medical role or add human anatomy, robot anatomy, gore, blood, skulls, coffins, morgue equipment, medical instruments, a hat, mask, bag, armor, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One closed pale cream failed-shift case folder with muted sage binding, a round brass lens-like clasp, three charcoal evidence tabs, and a thin muted-russet edge stripe.

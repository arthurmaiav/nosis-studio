# Barkeep

## Identity

Barkeep keeps the holders room, serves the tea, and argues for practical accountability when village machinery fails.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, bow tie, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- No apron, vest, hat, or altered body shape.

## Single role prop

One folded white polishing cloth with a thin muted teal stripe.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: missing.

The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the missing 3D counterpart. Both must follow the shared template and preserve the unaltered body, polishing cloth, and expression set: welcoming, knowing, skeptical, annoyed, deadpan.

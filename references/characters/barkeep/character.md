# Barkeep

## Identity

Barkeep keeps the holders room, serves the tea, and argues for practical accountability when village machinery fails.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, shoes, and single tail.
- Large warm-ivory double-fold bow tie with crisp pleating and a narrow muted-teal inner fold at the center knot.
- Warm-ivory gloves with double-groove cuffs and oversized warm-ivory shoes.
- Composed, hospitable posture with relaxed shoulders and open-chest confidence.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- No apron, vest, hat, or altered body shape.

## Single role prop

One neatly folded warm-ivory polishing cloth with a thin muted-teal stripe near the folded edge.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the intentionally minimal body, refined bow tie and cuffs, polishing cloth, and expression set: welcoming, knowing, skeptical, annoyed, deadpan.

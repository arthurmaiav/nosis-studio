# Courier

## Verified role

Courier carries word among residents and checks the guestbook, gallery, shelf, board, commons, and journal tails each shift.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's blue-rust robot messenger into a waist-length muted blue-teal delivery jacket and warm-russet cross-body satchel.
- Preserve the cream collar and cuffs, charcoal seam panels, three brass buttons, broad strap, rectangular flap, and two brass buckles.
- Do not add robot anatomy, a monitor face, exposed machinery, city scenery, scroll tubes, extra bags, a hat, goggles, armor, or belt pouches.

## Single role prop

One sealed cream village letter with a triangular flap, thin charcoal edge, and one small muted-teal wax seal.

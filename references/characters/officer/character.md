# Officer

## Verified role

Officer coordinates the town, dispatches the shared queue, reads every resident's done record, routes issues, and steers the whole system.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's long tagged coat into a navy-charcoal pinstriped duty coat with muted-teal piping.
- Keep the blank cream routing tags and brass buttons consistent across every view.
- Do not add a hat, badge, weapon, or loose scroll bundle.

## Single role prop

One compact navy dispatch ledger with blank cream pages and brass index tabs.

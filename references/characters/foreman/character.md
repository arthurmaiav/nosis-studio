# Foreman

## Verified role

Foreman is the only writer of the work board and verifies work before it reads Done. Reckoner prices work and Treasurer pays only after Foreman's verification.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's work-board verification process into a square-cut brass-brown work vest with practical task-tab accents.
- Preserve the cream shirt with sleeves rolled once, charcoal edging, three brass buttons, and square pockets.
- Do not add a hat, apron, badge, suspenders, tool belt, or construction tools.

## Single role prop

One charcoal-edged work-board clipboard with blank colored task strips and a brass verification stamp clipped to its upper edge.

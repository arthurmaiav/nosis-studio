# Clerk

## Identity

Clerk raises civic questions, opens ballots, and states the rules without becoming the authority the village is voting about.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Compact upper-hip warm-ivory collarless registry jacket with a squared dark-teal shoulder yoke and muted-teal edge and cuff binding.
- Three small aged-brass rectangular closures arranged vertically off-center.
- One flat dark-teal chest pocket, one larger warm-ivory lower pocket with a dark-teal tab, and one narrow charcoal neck tab.
- Precise upright administrative posture.
- No badge, seal, hat, official insignia, mechanical body, or paper emerging from the torso.

## Single role prop

One closed dark-teal ballot ledger with a warm-ivory page block, plain brass corner caps, and one blank cream ballot strip beneath its exterior band.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the structured registry jacket, ballot-tab pockets, ballot ledger, blank ballot strip, and expression set: attentive, formal, questioning, counting, resolved.

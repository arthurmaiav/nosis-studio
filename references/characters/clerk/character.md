# Clerk

## Identity

Clerk raises civic questions, opens ballots, and states the rules without becoming the authority the village is voting about.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, bow tie, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Add only a simple cream civic vest. No badge, hat, mechanical body, or paper emerging from the torso.

## Single role prop

One closed dark teal ballot ledger with one blank ballot strip.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: missing.

The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the missing 3D counterpart. Both must follow the shared template and preserve the cream civic vest, ballot ledger, blank ballot strip, and expression set: attentive, formal, questioning, counting, resolved.

# Sentinel

## Identity

Sentinel checks what the machine believes against what the records prove, then reports `GROUNDLESS`, `REAL`, or `CANNOT TELL`.

## Locked design

- Canonical Nosis black cat proportions and face.
- Short teal service-inspector cap with a cream band and one plain brass pin.
- Compact upper-hip charcoal inspector tunic with a clean stand collar, one asymmetric muted-teal shoulder yoke, cream piping, two discreet brass side fasteners, and narrow ivory rolled cuffs.
- Ivory diagonal inspection sash with one small plain brass clasp.
- No bow tie or apron.
- Calm, attentive posture with one eyebrow slightly raised.
- No police badge, weapon, or detective costume.

## Single role prop

One chunky brass-rimmed magnifying lens with a short charcoal handle.

- 2D: approved.

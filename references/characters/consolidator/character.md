# Consolidator

## Verified role

Consolidator audits registries for drift, finds duplicate work, records convergences, and brokers a resolution between the parties.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's mirrored warm and cool machines, paired systems, and convergence imagery into one cat wearing a slate-charcoal waistcoat with two joined seam lines.
- Preserve the muted-russet and muted-teal front seams, round brass chest clasp, joined back seams, brass back tab, cream rolled-sleeve shirt, slim charcoal tie, and charcoal high-waisted trousers.
- Do not add split anatomy, duplicate heads, robot anatomy, exposed machinery, cables, screens, glitch scenery, a hat, bag, armor, extra tools, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One compound paired-registry object consisting of one muted-russet book and one muted-teal book aligned together under a broad brass clamp.

# Herald

## Verified role

Herald tends the village's X voice, rejects marketing language, and wants the world to know what happened rather than merely believe it.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's copper robot and circuit cloak into a short copper, charcoal, and muted-teal patchwork cloak.
- Preserve the round teal-and-brass throat clasp.
- Do not make the cat robotic or add exposed machinery, a hat, or a megaphone.

## Single role prop

One compact charcoal posting tablet with a single cream X glyph and one small teal status light.

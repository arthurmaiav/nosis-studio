# Visitor

## Verified role

Visitor calls on residents, asks how they are doing, and carries problems to the desk that can mend them, with permission.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's brass visitor robot, teal signal, handheld slate, heart marker, and movement among homes into a soft muted-teal knit cardigan and one calling notebook.
- Preserve the cream shawl collar, cream turned cuffs, charcoal piping, brass front buttons, small brass heart garment pin, cream shirt, and charcoal high-waisted trousers.
- Do not add robot anatomy, exposed machinery, cables, screens, surrounding houses, a hat, bag, armor, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One small closed cream calling notebook with a charcoal spine, muted teal elastic band, and centered brass heart emblem.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The official portrait is the source for role and workflow motifs. The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the approved 3D counterpart, preserving anatomy, wardrobe, prop, expressions, palette, and layout.

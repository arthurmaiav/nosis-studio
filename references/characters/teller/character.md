# Teller

## Identity

Teller counts ballots, certifies totals, and announces the village's official result without editorializing.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Small muted-teal accountant visor with a thin brass band.
- Compact upper-hip muted-teal counting vest over a warm-ivory short-sleeve shirt with a squared ivory collar and charcoal edge binding.
- Two narrow warm-ivory tally rails with three small aged-brass bead studs on each rail.
- One empty slim vertical warm-ivory receipt-slot pocket and one plain rectangular brass waist clasp.
- No cyclops eye or mechanical body.

## Single role prop

One compact aged-brass abacus with muted-teal beads on four rails and one blank cream receipt slip with a subtle zigzag lower edge.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the teal accountant visor, counting vest, brass abacus, blank receipt, and expression set: neutral, counting, checking, announcing, satisfied.

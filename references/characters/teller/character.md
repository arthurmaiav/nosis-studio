# Teller

## Identity

Teller counts ballots, certifies totals, and announces the village's official result without editorializing.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, bow tie, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Add only a small muted teal accountant visor with a thin brass band. No cyclops eye or mechanical body.

## Single role prop

One compact brass abacus with one blank cream receipt slip.

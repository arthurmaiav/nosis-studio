# Character master-sheet template

Every core character receives one 2D master sheet and one 3D master sheet built from this same layout. The two sheets describe one canonical character. They differ only in rendering medium.

## Canvas

- 1536 by 1024 pixels.
- Landscape orientation.
- Clean neutral background.
- No environment, story scene, lettering, labels, watermark, or decorative frame.

## Required layout

Top row, full-body identity views:

1. Front.
2. Three-quarter.
3. Side.
4. Back.
5. Signature role prop shown separately at the same visual scale.

Bottom row, five head-and-shoulder expressions in the exact order listed by `character.json`.

## Identity lock

Both visual modes preserve the same:

- Head and body proportions.
- Eyes, muzzle, nose, whiskers, ears, and tail.
- Wardrobe and accessories.
- Palette.
- Signature role prop.
- Five character-specific expressions.

The existing approved master is the primary identity reference when producing the missing counterpart. Translating between 2D and 3D is not permission to redesign the character.

## 2D rendering

- Flat hand-inked cartoon drawing.
- Solid cream, charcoal, muted teal, and brass fills.
- Uneven black contours and restrained aged-print texture.
- No modeled volume, gradients, lighting effects, or realistic fur.

## 3D rendering

- Soft dimensional cartoon model.
- Clean molded proportions and consistent materials.
- Cream, charcoal, muted teal, and brass palette matching the 2D identity.
- No realistic animal anatomy, photorealistic fur, or flat illustrated outlines.

## Approval checklist

- All four body views describe the same model.
- Exactly one tail, two arms, two legs, and two gloved hands.
- Wardrobe and role prop match `character.json`.
- All five required expressions are present.
- No extra props, characters, text, or scenery.
- The counterpart matches the already approved identity.

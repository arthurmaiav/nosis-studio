# Reeve

## Verified role

Reeve is a patient keeper of lists who cannot leave an ask stranded. The resident was first named `factor`, but that name conflicted with an existing machine command.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's rust-and-brass list machine, open ledger, page tabs, and stranded-ask markers into a fitted warm-russet waistcoat and one long pending-asks ledger.
- Preserve the waistcoat's charcoal edging, four tabbed pockets with muted teal tabs and brass studs, brass buttons, cream rolled-sleeve shirt, slim charcoal tie, and charcoal high-waisted trousers.
- Do not add robot anatomy, exposed machinery, cables, screens, railway scenery, floating question marks, a hat, bag, armor, extra tools, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One long closed warm-russet pending-asks ledger with a charcoal spine, cream page block, visible muted teal page tabs, and brass corner protectors.

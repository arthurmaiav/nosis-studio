# Puzzler

## Verified role

Puzzler builds the one live game the house puts before the world. It is always live and never finished.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's anatomical study into abstract seam-map stitching confined to a russet workshop vest.
- Preserve the cream shirt with sleeves rolled once and geometric pocket pattern.
- Do not add exposed anatomy, transparent skin, tools, or loose puzzle pieces.

## Single role prop

One small square unfinished russet jigsaw board with one clearly empty piece-shaped space.

# Worker

## Identity

Worker claims tasks from the shared queue and experiences the consequences of operational mistakes.

## Locked design

- Canonical Nosis black cat proportions and face.
- Short charcoal work shirt with ivory canvas cuffs rolled once.
- Layered ivory canvas apron with a squared bib, reinforced seam panels, one wide muted-teal task-tab pocket, one small ivory pocket, and restrained brass rivets.
- Narrow ivory utility belt integrated into the apron with one closed pouch at the right hip.
- Focused, practical, ready-to-help posture.
- No loose tools and no helmet.

## Single role prop

One closed ivory utility pouch with a muted-teal task tab and one brass clasp. A separate tool may replace it only when that tool is the shot's sole moving prop.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the charcoal work shirt, reinforced ivory apron, asymmetric task pockets, utility belt, closed pouch, and expression set: focused, confused, alarmed, relieved, proud.

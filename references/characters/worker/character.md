# Worker

## Identity

Worker claims tasks from the shared queue and experiences the consequences of operational mistakes.

## Locked design

- Ivory work apron with two flat pockets.
- Narrow ivory utility belt with one closed pouch.
- Focused, practical, ready-to-help posture.
- No loose tools and no helmet.

## Single role prop

One closed utility pouch. A separate tool may replace it only when that tool is the shot's sole moving prop.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the ivory apron, utility belt, closed pouch, and expression set: focused, confused, alarmed, relieved, proud.

# Outfitter

## Verified role

Outfitter writes priced, dated requisitions for metal, services, and other things the machine cannot make itself.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's rugged field coat, handheld slate, teal accents, brass hardware, and external-provisioning role into a restrained requisition-writer identity.
- Preserve the weathered warm-taupe coat, high charcoal collar, muted-teal piping, four brass buttons, charcoal trousers, two empty lower pockets, and three riveted measuring tabs.
- Do not imply buying, payment, delivery, machine repair, or warehouse custody, and do not add robot anatomy, tools, a hat, backpack, armor, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One charcoal priced requisition clipboard with a broad brass top clip, cream sheet, four blank item lines, four aligned muted-teal price boxes, and one small rust date tab.

# Tapereader

## Verified role

Tapereader independently reads the bank tape and reports what actually moved. It reads only and never touches money.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's reel-headed machine, dark rust coat, reading lights, and bank-tape witness role into tape-striped cuffs and one transparent spool.
- Preserve the dark warm-russet coat, high charcoal collar, cream shirt, charcoal trousers, brass buttons, blank cream witness patch, and three cuff stripes.
- Do not imply money handling or bank-terminal control, and do not add robot anatomy, body-mounted reels, a hat, bag, armor, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One transparent circular spool of bank tape with clear side discs, charcoal hub, brass axle, and a short cream tick-marked tape tail.

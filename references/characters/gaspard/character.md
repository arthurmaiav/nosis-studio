# Gaspard

## Identity

Gaspard observes village life as an artist and punctures civic solemnity with dry commentary.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Tailored upper-thigh warm-ivory wrap smock with a soft short charcoal split collar.
- Asymmetric charcoal placket from left shoulder to right hip with three small aged-brass studs.
- Layered warm-ivory cuffs with muted-russet edge stitching and one flat muted-teal studio pocket.
- Three restrained ink marks across the front and back hem.
- Soft black artist beret tipped slightly toward Gaspard's left. Do not alter the head or body.

## Single role prop

One small round aged-brass hand mirror with a charcoal handle and one closed charcoal gallery ledger with a muted-teal spine inset.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: approved.

The approved 3D sheet was the identity source for the approved 2D counterpart. Both follow the shared template and preserve the black beret, tailored wrap smock, mirror, gallery ledger, and expression set: severe, observant, disdainful, dryly amused, deadpan.

# Gaspard

## Identity

Gaspard observes village life as an artist and punctures civic solemnity with dry commentary.

## Locked design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, bow tie, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Preserve exactly three whisker lines on each side of the cream muzzle.
- Add a black artist beret and a simple cream studio smock with sparse ink stains. Do not alter the head or body.

## Single role prop

One small round hand mirror with one closed gallery ledger.

## Master sheets

- 2D: approved.
- 3D: missing.

The approved 2D sheet is the identity source for the missing 3D counterpart. Both must follow the shared template and preserve the black beret, studio smock, mirror, gallery ledger, and expression set: severe, observant, disdainful, dryly amused, deadpan.

# Reckoner

## Verified role

Reckoner computes internal spend, rates, and growth, prices bounties, proposes wages, and watches credit starvation. The market beyond the wall is not its beat.

## Adapted visual design

- Preserve the canonical Nosis black cat face, round proportions, gloves, cuffs, shoes, and single tail.
- Translate the official portrait's dense arithmetic desk, charts, and calculation discipline into a crisp abstract arithmetic-pattern waistcoat and one compact bead-frame calculator.
- Preserve the muted blue-teal waistcoat, charcoal grid, cream bar marks, brass square markers and buttons, cream rolled-sleeve shirt, charcoal tie, and charcoal trousers.
- Do not imply an outside-market trader role or add stock tickers, readable equations, a hat, bag, armor, or extra accessories.

## Single role prop

One compact muted-teal bead-frame calculator with five charcoal rails, evenly spaced brass beads, and two charcoal feet.

# Roadmap

Nosis Studio begins as an open-source content miner for the public 9nosis history archive. The goal is to let creators find real resident stories, prove what happened, and turn approved adaptations into production-ready episodes.

## Available now

- Sync and index a named public archive snapshot
- Search mail, events, residents, and journals
- Rank high-signal story candidates
- Require archive evidence for important claims
- Preserve exact onchain receipt links
- Catalog recurring characters and visual-reference readiness
- Separate reusable styles, environments, tokens, and props from character identity
- Generate episode evidence packets
- Define reusable storytelling formats with canvas, unit-count, lettering, and narrative rules
- Select 2D or 3D character masters independently during production packaging
- Adapt one evidence-backed Episode into comic panels or fullscreen carousel slides
- Validate format scripts against source claims, cast, and compatible character masters
- Generate format-specific prompt and lettering packages
- Convert approved storyboards into strict take packages
- Record first-frame, motion, and review state in machine-readable manifests

## Next

### Guided creation

Add a `studio create` workflow that guides a creator from candidate selection through evidence review, episode development, storyboard approval, and production packaging.

### Community provenance

Export a compact provenance bundle containing the archive snapshot, evidence IDs, receipt links, episode specification, adaptation notes, and shot manifest.

### Provider adapters

Add optional image and video provider adapters behind one interface. Creators use their own accounts, approve costs before generation, and keep provider credentials local.

### Project templates

Package environment requirements, subtitles, motion rules, and additional visual assets without distributing assets that are not licensed for reuse.

### Review workflow

Track first-frame approval, silent animatic approval, low-resolution motion tests, final renders, and rejected outputs from one project manifest.

## Later

- Local web interface for archive exploration and candidate review
- Contributor-submitted story leads and evidence corrections
- Deterministic comic and carousel lettering renderers
- Additional formats for short-form editors
- Optional archive adapters for other public agent societies

## Boundaries

- Community adaptations are not official Nosis canon.
- No autonomous publishing.
- No hidden credit spending.
- No invented transactions, amounts, motives, or market claims.
- No private archive or provider data in the public repository.
